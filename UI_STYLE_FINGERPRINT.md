## 2. Macro-Composition Fingerprint (12 Mandatory Dimensions)

1. **Application Shell Archetype:**
   - **Interactive HTML5 Canvas 2D Spatial Simulator Viewport**: Full-bleed responsive 2D canvas with Retina `dpr` scaling, world coordinates `(50..990, 40..630)`, smooth pan/zoom transformation matrix, and camera centering. Enclosed within a restrained industrial chrome header and collapsible inspector drawer.

2. **Navigation Model:**
   - **Spatial Camera Tracking & Quick-Filter Pill Ribbon**: Top command bar featuring room filter pills (`Full Campus`, `Manager Suite`, `Dev Wing`, `Research Lab`, `Conference`, `QA & Security`, `Lounge`, `Servers`) that trigger animated camera pans with zoom interpolation, coupled with direct world-space canvas hit-testing to click and track autonomous agent avatars.

3. **Viewport Zoning & Grid Rhythm:**

## 4. Paket B — Japandi Digital Atelier Spatial Transformation (Fleet V3.3)

**Engine:** Three.js WebGL 3D Spatial Canvas (`RobloxOffice3D.ts` + `OfficeFloorPlan.ts`)  
**Design Authority:** AURORA (Art Direction) & LENS (Rendered Runtime QA)  
**Implementation Engineer:** FRAME (Principal Frontend Experience Engineer)  
**Execution Date:** September 29, 2026  

### Transformation Pillars

1. **Frosted Glass Capsule Pill (Anti-Comic Cloud Refinement):**
   - Replaced oversized 9.2 x 4.2 comic book cloud balloons and cartoon descending circles with a precision visionOS-inspired glass capsule pill (3.4 x 0.95 3D units).
   - High-DPI texture resolution: 1024 x 280 px with 36px corner radius.
   - Surface Material: Dark Obsidian Slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.88)`), subtle top-down specular rim highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), and 1.2px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
   - Typography: Crisp `#F8FAFC` high-contrast header (WCAG AAA >= 14:1) with micro-pill status tag and muted `#94A3B8` activity description.
   - Interactive Focus Ring: Agent-colored glowing outline (`rgba(color, 0.45)`) when selected by user.

2. **State-Driven Density ("The Quiet Room" Mode) & War Room Radial Exclusion:**
   - **IDLE / Standby State:** Suppresses giant text billboards entirely. Only a 6–8px equivalent Luminous Micro-Dot floats above the avatar head with gentle breathing pulse animation (`sin(elapsed * 2.0)`).
   - **WORKING / Meeting State:** Smooth cubic spring lerp transition expanding micro-dot to the compact frosted capsule pill.
   - **War Room Radial Exclusion Zone:** The central 2.5m radius circle around the War Room conference table and central A2A 3D hologram is strictly guarded against text occlusion. Idle and seated agents do not project high overhead tags into the camera sightline, guaranteeing the cyan wireframe hologram is 100% visible and unobstructed from all 360° orbit angles.

3. **Dark Void Canvas (#0B0F17) + Multi-Tier Beveled Floating Slab:**
   - `scene.background = new THREE.Color(0x0B0F17)` and `scene.fog = new THREE.FogExp2(0x0B0F17, 0.0018)`.
   - Eliminates visual "contrast sandwich" between bright canvas and dark dashboard chrome (#0A0D14 / #11141A).
   - Floating Architectural Plinth: 4-tier beveled slab crafted from Dark Basalt (`#111622`) and Roasted Walnut (`#231A14`), elevated over a 92 x 72m soft radial contact shadow plane (`Y = -1.02m`) to establish a museum-grade floating diorama presence.

4. **Living Props & Workstation Illumination:**
   - **Coffee Steam Particle System:** Single batched `THREE.Points` (32 particles across 4 cups: Lounge barista espresso machine, Lounge table, Dev Lab desk, Executive desk) rising with sinusoidal drift and soft alpha fade out. Warm amber-white tint (`#FDE68A`). Exactly 1 draw call, 0 performance impact.
   - **Workstation Task Glow:** Active agents trigger a 2700K warm amber glow (`#FFAE5D` / `#FFB86C`) across their desk surface and dual monitors without dynamic point-light shadow penalties.
